# Playbook — Unknown / unclassified

Conventions for an **unknown** break. The procedure — gather what is cheaply available, summarise known
versus missing, escalate rather than propose — is in the `unknown` skill. This is what makes an
escalation useful to the analyst who receives it.

**Name the missing dimension, never "no match found".** An unclassified item is nearly always missing a
specific thing: no fund on the notice, no usable date, an amount that is a facility total with no
allocation, an identifier in a namespace nothing links to, or only an issuer name. The analyst's next
action differs completely for each. "Insufficient evidence" tells them nothing they did not know from
the item landing in their queue.

**Incomplete documents are their own category.** A truncated fax or a scan with missing pages is not an
ambiguous document — it is an incomplete one, and the useful escalation says which pages are absent so
someone can request them. Do not infer the missing content from the pages that arrived.

**An item with several indistinguishable candidates is also unknown.** Say how many candidates there
were and what failed to separate them; that is what lets an analyst pick, and it is more useful than a
ranked guess.
