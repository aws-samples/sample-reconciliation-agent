Hi Daniel,

Confirming what we discussed. The USD 9,640.18 receipt you are showing on 2026-03-02 settled on our books with a value date of 2026-03-04. This is not an amount difference — both sides agree on the amount to the cent. It is a two business day settlement offset, which is the standard behaviour for this counterparty on USD loan interest.

Coastline applies the agent's stated value date, not the payment initiation date. Meridian Agency Services initiate on the payment due date shown on the advice, and the funds land T+2 through the Tarnsmoor correspondent chain, so you will see this offset on every Cindermoor Logistics interest payment. It is within the expected settlement window for the facility and no action is required — the break clears itself once the later side posts.

Worth noting for the item you queried: the 2026-03-02 payment was a consolidated wire covering three facilities, and the offset applies to the wire, so all three components move together. Do not chase the TL-A leg on its own.

For reference, the offsets we have observed on this counterparty over the last two quarters:

  • 2025-12-02 interest payment — 2 business days
  • 2026-01-05 principal repayment — 2 business days
  • 2026-01-20 interest payment — 3 business days (2026-01-19 was a US settlement holiday)
  • 2026-03-02 interest payment — 2 business days (this item)

The one thing worth flagging: if you ever see an offset of more than three business days on this counterparty, that is outside the window and is worth escalating rather than ageing. Twice last year that turned out to be a genuinely failed payment that had been re-initiated, not a timing difference at all.

Our standard settlement calendar for the Cindermoor facilities is on the SharePoint site if you need to confirm a specific date.
