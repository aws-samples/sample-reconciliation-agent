Three things that have caused allocation errors on previous consolidated advices and are worth calling out:

  1. The wire reference does not identify a facility. The advice quotes CINDERMOOR LOGISTICS HOLDINGS, INC. $850MM 3/15/2021 — that is the credit agreement, and all three components carry it. Matching this wire on its reference returns three candidates by construction. The per-facility Cash Amount is the discriminator, not the reference.
  2. The TL-B component is a principal paydown, not interest, even though it is remitted in the same wire as two interest payments. It must not be included in any interest accrual reconciliation.
  3. The advice prints a Total Interest Amount beside each Cash Amount — 470,250.00 on the revolver and 8,265,552.00 on TL-A. Those are facility-wide figures for all lenders. Only the Cash Amount is ours. Booking a Total Interest Amount as a receivable is the error that produced the four-figure-to-seven-figure mismatch on the November advice.
