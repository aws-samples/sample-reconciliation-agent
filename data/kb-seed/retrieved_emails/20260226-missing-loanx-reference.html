The notice carries no LoanX identifier, and that is expected rather than an omission on our side. This facility was novated to us from a predecessor agent on 2026-01-14 and LoanX has not yet reissued the identifier against our facility record. Until it does, the notice will keep going out without it.

Match on the two identifiers the notice does carry:

  • Facility ID SL-99001 — stable across the novation
  • Reference WIRE-20260302-EVG — unique to this payment

To be explicit, because this has been misread before: the absence of the LoanX ID is not an amount discrepancy and should not be aged as one. Recover the facility from SL-99001, then match on the amount — it will tie to the cent.

Do not carry the predecessor agent's LoanX ID forward. That identifier belongs to the retired facility record, and reusing it will point your position at a facility we no longer administer. Wait for the reissued value.

For the avoidance of doubt, the identifiers currently in force for this borrower are:

  • CINDERMOOR LOGISTICS TL-B $250MM — Facility ID SL-99001 — LoanX: not yet reissued
  • CINDERMOOR LOGISTICS TL-A $160MM — LoanXid as printed: LX223061XXXX1
  • CINDERMOOR REVOLVING CREDIT FACILITY $200MM — Facility ID SL-204833

One trap on that middle line, since it bites every time a new facility is onboarded. A LoanX ID as printed on a notice is not always the value you match on. Our notices state the rule themselves: a valid LoanX ID is LX followed by six digits, and where the printed string ends in a run of Xs the first eight characters are the valid value. So LX223061XXXX1 is LX223061. Matching the printed thirteen-character string against a reference file holding the eight-character one produces a missing-reference break on a facility that is in fact fully mapped.

We will confirm by email once LoanX reissues the TL-B identifier. Treat that as a mapping update, not a second payment.
